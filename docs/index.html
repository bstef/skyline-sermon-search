From podcast to passage

A weekly rhythm, behind the scenes.

The website stays fast while audio processing happens on a separate local host.

  1. 01

    Discover

    n8n checks the public podcast feed and selects a bounded batch of untranscribed episodes.

    Podcast RSS + n8n
  2. 02

    Prepare

    Python and FFmpeg download and split the audio into 10-minute, mono FLAC chunks in the LXC.

    Proxmox LXC
  3. 03

    Transcribe

    Groq returns timestamped text. Completed chunks are cached, and transcripts stay on the local host.

    Groq Whisper
  4. 04

    Publish

    A protected upload updates Cloudflare D1 and keyword search, without rebuilding the website.

    Workers + D1

Can I listen before a sermon is transcribed?

Yes. Episode metadata and original audio are available independently of transcripts. Passage search becomes available after actual transcript text is published.

Does Groq also provide meaning-based search?

Groq handles speech recognition. Meaning-based search is a separate optional embeddings integration. Without that configuration, smart search visibly falls back to keyword retrieval.

What stays on the LXC?

Transcript JSON, chunk-response checkpoints, the local SQLite index, and publication receipts. Downloaded audio and converted chunks are temporary and removed when processing exits normally.

How do retries avoid paying twice?

The runner skips indexed sermons and resumes saved chunk responses. Publication has separate receipts, so a failed upload can retry the existing transcript without another Groq transcription call.
